# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration space for everyone, regardless of
background, identity, experience, or level of technical familiarity.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and project boundaries.
- Accept correction gracefully and keep consequential decisions auditable.
- Avoid harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, doxxing, or retaliation.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through GitLab. Reports will be reviewed fairly
and kept as confidential as practical. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, restrict
participation, or ban contributors whose behavior is unsafe or persistently disruptive.
